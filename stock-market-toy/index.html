How this works Agents drop BUY/SELL orders into the book during each window. A limit order sets a price limit; a market order accepts any price. At the end of the window the market picks the one price where the most shares can trade, matches buyers (willing to pay ≥ price) with sellers (willing to accept ≤ price), and that price becomes the new price of STOCK. Leftover limit orders stay in the book for a few rounds. Try the whale buttons, or place your own orders, and watch the price react. Negative shares = short position. Unlimited capital is assumed.
